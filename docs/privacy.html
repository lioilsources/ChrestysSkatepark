Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Overview

This policy covers Skatepark: Chřestýš (in-app title RattleRamp), a skate game by Oldřich Vořechovský, on the App Store and Google Play as com.ol1n.chrestysSkatepark. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or the advertising ID
  • Usage analytics, crash reports or telemetry
  • Photos, contacts, microphone or camera input

No Network, No Storage

The game runs entirely on your device and makes no network requests. It has no accounts, leaderboards, ads or in-app purchases. Scores, the chosen rider and settings live only in memory while the app is open and are discarded when it closes; nothing is written to the device and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Third-Party Services

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Downloads and updates are handled by the App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes – for example if a future version adds saved scores or online features – the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.

Česky – Zásady ochrany soukromí

Aplikace Skatepark: Chřestýš nesbírá, neukládá ani nesdílí žádné osobní údaje. Nekomunikuje po síti, nemá účty, žebříčky, reklamy ani nákupy a neobsahuje žádné analytické ani sledovací SDK. Skóre, zvolený jezdec a nastavení existují jen v paměti po dobu běhu aplikace a po zavření se zahodí.
