How to Play

  • Tap the screen to start, pick a rider and tap JEĎ! (or wait 5 seconds).
  • Draw on the 3×3 grid in the bottom right: around the edge = spin (clockwise is frontside), through the centre = grab, the diamond = flip.
  • Lift your finger when the shrinking ring meets the coping. Releasing too early is ignored; too late or an unknown pattern means a bail.
  • Chain tricks in one stroke: each one starts where the previous ended.
  • TRICK BOOK on the title screen shows the pattern of every trick; PRO ×1,5 starts a ride without hints for more points.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my scores saved?

Not yet. Each ride ends with a score card, but scores are kept only until the next ride. There is no cloud account.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. It works fully offline.

Why is the game in Czech?

It was made for a bar in the Czech Republic. Trick names and timing calls use the usual English skate terms.

Can I change the look?

Yes – the STYL button on the title screen switches between neon, comic and pixel art.
